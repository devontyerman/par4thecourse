Featured Courses

Legendary Courses, One Swing Away.

From the windswept links of Scotland to the coastal cliffs of California — play them all on TrackMan at Par 4 The Course.

⛳

St Andrews Old Course

St Andrews, Scotland

The birthplace of golf. This legendary links course has hosted The Open Championship 30 times and remains the most iconic course in the world.

⛳

Pebble Beach Golf Links

Pebble Beach, California

One of the most iconic coastal golf courses in the world, known for its dramatic ocean views and legendary tournament history.

⛳

Augusta National Golf Club

Augusta, Georgia

Home of The Masters. Experience Amen Corner, the azalea-lined fairways, and the most prestigious course in competitive golf.

⛳

TPC Sawgrass

Ponte Vedra Beach, Florida

Home of THE PLAYERS Championship and the famous island green 17th hole — one of the most recognized par 3s in golf.

⛳

Bethpage Black

Farmingdale, New York

A brutally challenging public course that has hosted the U.S. Open and PGA Championship. Known for its famous warning sign at the first tee.

⛳

Royal Birkdale

Southport, England

One of the finest links courses in England's Open Championship rotation, with towering dunes and challenging coastal winds.

⛳

Torrey Pines

La Jolla, California

Perched on cliffs overlooking the Pacific Ocean, this municipal gem hosts the Farmers Insurance Open and has staged the U.S. Open.

⛳

Pinehurst No. 2

Pinehurst, North Carolina

A Donald Ross masterpiece in the Sandhills. Its crowned, turtle-back greens make it one of the most demanding tests in American golf.

⛳

Muirfield Village

Dublin, Ohio

Designed by Jack Nicklaus and home of the Memorial Tournament. A meticulously maintained course that tests every part of your game.

⛳

Harbour Town Golf Links

Hilton Head Island, South Carolina

Famous for its iconic lighthouse and tight, tree-lined fairways. Home of the RBC Heritage and a favorite among PGA Tour players.
