Why TrackMan?

Not All Simulators Are Equal.

There are plenty of simulator brands out there — but TrackMan is in a league of its own. Here's how it stacks up against competitors like SkyTrak, Garmin Approach, FlightScope, and others.

🏌 TrackMan
Other Simulators
✓ Dual radar technology — tracks both club and ball with military-grade precision
✗ Camera-based or single radar — less accurate, affected by lighting conditions
✓ Used by 98% of PGA Tour pros, top coaches, and major equipment fitters worldwide
✗ Limited or no professional tour adoption — primarily consumer-grade devices
✓ 40+ data parameters per shot — ball speed, spin axis, launch angle, club path, attack angle, and more
✗ Typically 10–20 metrics — many are estimated or interpolated rather than directly measured
✓ ±1 yard carry distance accuracy — the gold standard that all other systems are measured against
✗ ±3–10 yard variance common — spin and distance readings can drift significantly
✓ Works flawlessly indoors and outdoors — same hardware used on Tour driving ranges
✗ Many systems are indoor-only or require different hardware/calibration for outdoor use
✓ 180+ courses including 43 real Tour venues with high-fidelity graphics and accurate elevation data
✗ Smaller course libraries — many courses are computer-generated rather than laser-scanned
✓ Full club data measured directly — face angle, club path, dynamic loft, and impact location
✗ Club data often estimated from ball flight or requires additional sensors/stickers
✓ Direct spin measurement via radar — accurate backspin, sidespin, and spin axis on every shot
✗ Spin often calculated from ball flight algorithms — unreliable on short game and partial shots
✓ Built-in competitive modes — closest-to-the-pin, long drive, stroke play, and virtual tournaments
✗ Basic game modes — many lack real-time leaderboard and multi-player features
✓ Zero calibration needed — powered on and ready to go in seconds with consistent readings every session
✗ Frequent recalibration required — readings can drift over time and between sessions

About the Owner

Meet Tyson.
Born & Raised in Pinellas.

Growing up in the St. Pete and Clearwater area, Tyson developed a love for golf at an early age. What started with weekend rounds at local munis and high school golf team practices turned into a lifelong passion for the game and the community it builds.

After years of traveling to find high-end simulator experiences, Tyson's vision became clear: bring world-class golf technology home to Pinellas County. Par 4 The Course was built on his belief that everyone deserves access to the same tools the pros use — in a space that feels welcoming whether you're a scratch golfer or swinging for the very first time.

"I wanted to build the place I always wished existed in my hometown — somewhere anyone can walk in, grab a drink, and fall in love with golf."
